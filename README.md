# my-first-game
This is a smartphone game I created as a practice project.
You can play the game by opening the address shown in the deployment details—provided below—on your Android device, iPhone, or computer.
https://yoshiota54-cmyk.github.io/my-first-game/

Here's how to play.

Opening the link above will take you to a page featuring links to four different games. Click the link for the game you wish to play, then tap the "TOUCH to START" button to begin.

game1
The female character is the player and the bear is the enemy.
Tap the direction you want the player to move. When an enemy comes, tap the player himself,
Make it jump. If you can hit the enemy, you get 100 points. If you touch an enemy without jumping, minus 10 points.
If you can hit the item in the bottle, you will get 300 points. (If the bottle is in the air, make it jump.
Game over after a certain amount of time.

game2
This is a 2D role-playing game where you play as a female character, and the enemies are bears.
Tap the direction you want your character to move. (There is no jumping.)
Bump into enemies or items to interact with them. Pink bears are strong, so make sure to hit them with an item first before bumping into them.
Defeat the enemies and reach the spot marked with a picture of stairs to advance to the next game screen.
Continue in the same way, and once you reach the spot with the stairs, you will clear the game.

game3
A quiz game. Questions will appear in the red area; please select your answer.
The game will indicate whether your answer is correct or incorrect. Tap the red area to proceed to the next question.
There are 5 questions in total, and you will receive a final evaluation at the end.

game4 [Cannot be played on a PC; this game is controlled by tilting your smartphone.]
Tilt your smartphone to the right to move the player (a female character) to the right.
Tilt it to the left to move her to the left. Tilt it toward you to move her downward, and tilt it away from you (tilting the screen upward) to move her upward. Make good use of the two trampolines.
Reach the top of the ladder at the very end to clear the game; if you fall, it’s game over.

Each game screen features a "メニューに戻る" button; tapping it takes you back to the initial game selection screen, where you can choose to play a different game.
